## Brand & Style

This design system targets college students, recent graduates, and career mentors preparing for competitive recruitment drives, technical assessments, and interviews. The design narrative centers on **clarity, momentum, and calm ambition**. Placement preparation is high-stakes and stressful; the interface must feel structured, empowering, and friction-free, translating overwhelming roadmaps into bite-sized daily triumphs.

The aesthetic blends **Modern Tactile Minimalism** with **Educational SaaS precision**:
- **Structured Clarity:** Heavy reliance on clear visual cards, modular dashboards, and high typographic legibility to lower cognitive load during late-night study sessions.
- **Dynamic Progression:** Vibrant milestone indicators, circular completion rings, and clean telemetry badges that reward consistency without feeling childish.
- **Mobile Ergonomics:** Thumb-friendly interactive zones, floating bottom control centers, and high contrast compliant with real-world mobile lighting conditions (lecture halls, commutes, dorms).

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative corporate depth with fresh, motivational highlights:

- **Primary (`#3B82F6` - Royal Electric Blue):** Represents momentum, primary interactions, active tab states, and major progress triggers.
- **Secondary (`#1E293B` - Deep Indigo Slate):** Anchors navigation bars, key headers, technical metrics, and high-impact structural containers.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` - Emerald Growth):** Dedicated to success validation, streak counts, completed mock interviews, and DSA topic mastery.
- **Accent Warning (`#F59E0B` - Amber Focus):** Used for interview countdowns, urgent deadlines, weak-topic warnings, and review flags.
- **Canvas & Card Neutrals (`#F8FAFC`, `#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity, cool-tinted slate backdrops preventing optical fatigue while maintaining separation from pure white elevated cards.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E2E8F0`):** Subdued hairline structural boundaries that maintain container definition without visual noise.
- **Text & Content Hierarchy (`#0F172A` Slate 900, `#475569` Slate 600, `#94A3B8` Slate 400):** High-contrast, WCAG AAA compliant text tones for effortless reading under varied mobile screen brightness.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all layers. Its modern geometric curves provide an approachable, student-friendly tone while its clean apertures and distinct numerals guarantee high readability for technical problem counts, timer countdowns, and performance percentages.

- **Headlines:** Use heavy weights (700 to 800) with slight negative tracking to give milestones and interview readiness scores bold editorial punch.
- **Body:** Kept balanced at 400 and 500 weight with generous line-heights to support comfortable scanning of technical questions, aptitude explanations, and company round details.
- **Labels & Badges:** Use semi-bold and bold weights with subtle letter-spacing for status tags, company tags, and tabular metrics.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a standard **8pt base grid** optimized for modern handheld viewport constraints (360px to 428px wide mobile viewports):

- **Mobile Canvas Margins:** Strict 16px (`1rem`) outer gutters ensure safe touch distance from phone bezels while maximizing card content width.
- **Section Rhythm:** Vertical content sections adhere to 24px (`1.5rem`) gaps, creating clear cognitive zones between Daily Goals, Company Trackers, and Readiness Matrices.
- **Micro-Gaps:** Icon-label pairings and nested badge data use 4px (`space-xs`) and 8px (`space-sm`) gaps to maintain tight visual grouping.
- **Adaptability:** On larger viewports (tablets or foldables), the layout transitions into a multi-column card grid with expanded 24px margins, keeping the primary tracking flow centered with a maximum content line-length constraint of 640px.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system uses **Tonal Layering combined with Soft Ambient Diffusion** to establish clean visual layers without muddying the interface:

- **Canvas (Level 0):** Pure `#F8FAFC` base surface providing a calm, non-glare foundation.
- **Raised Surfaces (Level 1 - Cards, List Tiles):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a hairline `1px solid #E2E8F0` border and elevated with a diffuse shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Interactive & Focused Cards (Level 2 - Active Topic, Active Mock):** Elevated with a subtle blue-tinted drop shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.12), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Modals, Drawers & Bottom Sheets (Level 3):** Grounded by a backdrop scrim (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)`) with a deep ambient elevation: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`.
- **No Harsh Outlines:** Never use high-contrast heavy black borders; borders must always blend with the neutral surface hierarchy.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture emphasizes modern rounded comfort to make career tracking feel encouraging rather than bureaucratic:

- **Primary Cards:** Employ `rounded-2xl` (16px) or `rounded-3xl` (24px) for hero progress cards, mock interview summaries, and streak displays.
- **Interactive Controls:** Buttons, text inputs, and search bars use uniform `rounded-xl` (12px) radii for ergonomic finger taps.
- **Badges, Chips & Progress Indicators:** Use fully rounded pill shapes (`border-radius: 9999px`) to distinguish categorical metadata and telemetry from rectangular content cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Deep royal blue (`#3B82F6`) background, pure white text, semi-bold weight, `rounded-xl`, height 48px to 52px for accessibility. Subtle scale-down transform (0.98) on active press.
- **Secondary Button:** Surface `#FFFFFF` with `#E2E8F0` border and `#1E293B` text.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** No background, `#3B82F6` text, used for secondary actions like "View All Questions" or "Skip for now".

### Badges & Pill Chips
- **Status Badges:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), 24px height, horizontal padding 10px.
  - *Completed / Solved:* Background `#ECFDF5`, text and icon `#059669`.
  - *In Review / In Progress:* Background `#FEF3C7`, text and icon `#D97706`.
  - *High Priority:* Background `#FEE2E2`, text and icon `#DC2626`.
- **Filter Chips:** Inactive in `#FFFFFF` with `#E2E8F0` border; active toggled to `#1E293B` with white label.

### Progress Gauges (Circular & Linear)
- **Linear Track:** 8px track height, rounded pill caps. Background track `#E2E8F0`, filled track using `#10B981` or gradient `#3B82F6` to `#10B981`.
- **Circular Readiness Wheel:** Minimum 84px diameter for mobile dashboards. SVG stroke-width 8px, background track `#E2E8F0`, active stroke animated in `#10B981`, with bold center-anchored percentage typography.

### Input Fields
- **Search & Text Inputs:** 48px height, `#FFFFFF` fill, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, `rounded-xl`, 16px horizontal padding. Left-aligned subtle slate icon (`#94A3B8`). Focus ring: `2px solid #3B82F6` with an optical offset of 2px.

### List Items & Topic Rows
- Containerized list items on `#FFFFFF` with 12px vertical padding, separated by `#F1F5F9` dividers or stacked as individual soft cards with 8px margin-bottom. Trailing action includes status indicator pill or chevron navigation cue.

### Checkboxes & Check Rings
- Rounded squares (6px radius) or circular completion dots (22px diameter). Unchecked state uses `#CBD5E1` border; checked state features a snappy scale transition into `#10B981` with a crisp white checkmark icon.

### Bottom Navigation Bar
- Fixed mobile navigation container: `#FFFFFF` surface, height 64px + safe area inset, border-top `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Active tab displays filled icon in `#3B82F6` with a micro active-dot indicator beneath; inactive tabs render in `#64748B`.
